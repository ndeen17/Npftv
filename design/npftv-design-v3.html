NPFTV demo: design direction

Current NPFTV brand with layout ideas from the Sheriff's Office design. All content is demo data.

01

NPFTV website: homepage

Chosen hero: the NPFTV emblem, with no photo. Straight after the quick-access row, a Live on NPFTV Stream module embeds the live channel and links to Stream. The rest follows the inspiration: alert bar, director's message, news beside upcoming broadcasts, programmes, public safety, photo band, social feed, footer.

02

NPFTV Stream: its own product

Stream has its own home, navigation and address (stream.npftv.net), and works on its own. It stays connected to NPFTV: Watch live and the homepage module lead here, it links back to npftv.net, and both share the same programmes, schedule, design and Control Room.

03

Control Room: AI copy desk

An editor reviews a story from the Kano correspondent. AI drafts and flags; the editor decides.

04

Control Room: Network Director

The ERP view. One press briefing shows up in crew, assets, budget and approvals, with an AI daily brief on top.

Hero options not chosen
