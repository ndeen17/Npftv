AI scam checker

Is this message a scam?

Paste a text, WhatsApp or email message. We'll point out the warning signs in plain language. Nothing you paste is stored.

AI can make mistakes. If in doubt, call your bank on the number on your card.
Very likely a scam3 warning signs
  • It rushes you with a 24-hour deadline.
  • The link doesn't belong to your bank or the Central Bank.
  • It asks for your OTP. No bank or police officer will ever ask for it.

What to do: don't reply or click the link. Delete the message and tell your bank.
